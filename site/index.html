How it works

It lives in your menu bar. No typing, no formatting, no account.

01

Capture

Hold ⌥⇧Space or click the menu-bar icon and brain-dump. Words appear as you talk.

02

Organize

Tasks go straight onto your Today list. Topics, people and goals join your mind map.

03

Search

Find anything you've said by meaning. "Car stuff" finds "the car needs servicing".

04

Meetings

On a call? Nodemind notes what you and they said, then writes the minutes. Your action items land on Today.

How fast?

Everything runs on your Mac's own chip. Speech is transcribed at every pause while you talk, so there's almost nothing left to do when you stop.

StepModelTime after you stop
Speech to textWhisper small whisper.cpp, Metal, live at each pause~0.2 s
Checklist, topics, peopleQwen3.5 0.8B llama.cpp, Metal, passes in parallel~2–4 s
Search indexEmbeddingGemma 2 semantic memory search< 0.1 s

Measured on an M4 Pro MacBook with a 65-second English recording: each 8–12 s piece transcribes in ~0.24 s while you're still talking. Older Macs take longer.

Install

  1. Download

    Get Nodemind-macOS.dmg from the button above.

  2. Drag to Applications

    Open the DMG and drag Nodemind into your Applications folder.

  3. Open it the first time

    Nodemind isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time, right-click the app → Open → Open. After that it opens normally.

  4. Let it set up

    Nodemind downloads its AI models once (1.6 GB), then lives in your menu bar. Approve the microphone the first time you record.

Questions

Do I need internet?

Only once, to download the AI models on first launch. After that Nodemind works fully offline; the only other network call is an optional check for updates.

Which languages does it understand?

English, and Hinglish (Hindi mixed with English). Switch to Hinglish under the mic button. Your checklist is always written in English.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Not yet. Nodemind needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer).

How do meeting notes work?

Click "Take meeting notes" in the menu bar before a call. Nodemind records your mic and the call audio playing on your Mac separately, so it knows who said what, with no bot joining your call. When you stop, it writes a summary, decisions and action items, all on your Mac. Needs macOS 14.2 or newer; headphones give the cleanest notes. Let others know you're taking notes.

Is it really free?

Yes, and open source under the MIT license. There's no paid tier.

How do updates work?

Nodemind checks GitHub for new versions and asks before installing one.
